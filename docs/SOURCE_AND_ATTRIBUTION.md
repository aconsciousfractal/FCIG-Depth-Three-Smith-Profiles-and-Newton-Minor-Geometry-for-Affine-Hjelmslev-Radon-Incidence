# Source and attribution boundary

The exact bibliography is `paper/references.bib`. No cited paper, database
export or third-party source tree is redistributed.

## Imported ingredients

- Weber--Künzer and Mahatab--Sampath supply cyclic integral and CRT
  elementary-divisor ingredients used in the top-shell analysis.
- Lipman and Lipman--Watanabe supply complete-ideal, antinef-unloading and
  Hoskin--Deligne length machinery used in the modular-rank proof.
- Sagan supplies q-Lucas; Cai--Ehrenborg--Readdy record the relevant integral
  q-Stirling/Carlitz expansion.
- Standard Smith-form, cyclotomic and finite-module ingredients are cited at
  their points of use.
- The author's depth-two paper supplies the integral recursive top-shell dual
  identity used in the divided-transfer criterion; the present manuscript
  states the exact point where that input enters and proves the new
  depth-three obstruction calculation.

The paper states how each imported theorem enters. Those results remain the
work of their authors.

## Adjacent, nontransferred literature

Łaba--Trainor and Dvir concern the modular hyperplane-function object and
rank bounds; their work is not used to infer the integral Smith profile.
Landjev, Bajalan--Landjev--Rousseva, Kohnert and the broader projective
Hjelmslev literature concern adjacent projective settings; no projective
incidence matrix is identified with the affine matrix here.

For records available only at metadata or abstract level, no inaccessible
theorem is imported. Limited access is a ceiling on originality statements,
not evidence of novelty.

## Manuscript-specific arguments

The tripotent-sector common-image reduction, exact q-Lucas block product,
boundary-generation recurrence, Newton-minors theorem, threshold inversion,
typed cross-chart divided carry and complete depth-three affine Smith profile
are proved in the manuscript. The code checks their displayed consequences;
it is not the proof.

Stable DOIs and public locators are recorded in the bibliography. Missing
DOIs are not invented.

# Public claim boundary

## Affirmative mathematical statements

The paper proves, for every prime `p`, the complete `p`-primary Smith
profile of the depth-three affine point--line incidence matrix stated in its
abstract and main theorem. It proves the exponent is `p^5`, gives all six
multiplicities on the two odd-prime residue classes modulo six, and treats
`p=2,3` separately.

It also proves the exact truncated q-Pascal common-image theorem, the
q-Newton determinantal-minor formula, the resulting Hilbert and Fitting data,
the minimal generator count, and the typed cross-chart divided-carry theorem.
The finite companion supplies supporting computational checks only.

## Explicit nonclaims

This package does not claim:

- novelty, firstness or priority for any statement;
- a literature-wide or exhaustive source search;
- a closed Smith profile at arbitrary depth;
- a general standard-basis, Rees or strictness theorem for filtered
  intersections;
- that finite calculations prove an all-prime theorem;
- that adjacent projective Hjelmslev results determine the affine integer
  Smith profile;
- a projective-to-affine incidence-matrix identification;
- tagged-PDF or PDF/UA conformance;
- human, institutional or model-family independence for any machine review.

## Computational scope

The main companion validates the structured all-prime formula, symbolic rank
and weighted-order identities, prime controls and Newton counts. The bounded
exceptional packages reconstruct only `p=2,3`: two Smith algorithms, a
primal/dual first-transfer certificate, a direct `p=2` two-chart rank
certificate for `epsilon_1(2,3)=1`, and a `p=3` cross-chart certificate.
None is represented as a substitute for the uniform proof or as an
all-prime interpolation.

Readers should cite the paper for mathematical statements and describe the
software as exact reproducibility and mutation-sensitive cross-checking.

The mathematical-development provenance and author-responsibility boundary
are stated in `AI_USE.md` and in the paper itself.
